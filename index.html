Emergency tree removal

If a tree has come down, or you're worried it might, call right away. Our team responds to emergency requests, assesses the danger and works quickly to remove it and secure the area, including debris clearing and emergency trimming.

Tree removal

Whenever possible we care for a tree before turning to removal. When a tree does need to come down, we do it safely and make sure it becomes mulch for future soil health.

Tree trimming & pruning

Your pruning options include:

  • Cleaning: removing dying and diseased branches from the crown
  • Thinning: selecting branches to improve structure and air movement
  • Raising: removing lower branches for clearance over structures
  • Reduction: reducing size while keeping the tree's form
Stump grinding & removal

Tired of mowing around an old stump? Grinding turns it into wood chips that make mulch for your grass and soil, so the spot can be covered with dirt instead of digging up the whole root system.

FAQ

Common tree service questions

Is tree work expensive?

Prices vary with the size of the tree and the work required. Obstacles like structures, wires, fences and other plants affect the cost most. Call for a quote.

How can I get a tree service estimate?

Call (416) 617-5804 or send the form below. We'll set up an on-site consultation to give you an accurate estimate.

Why is stump grinding necessary?

Grinding removes the stump without digging up the large root system. The chips make mulch, and the spot can be covered with soil while the rest decays.

When should cabling and bracing be used?

When limbs are at risk of failing. Cabling can protect a weak tree, preserve its look, or keep it from falling over a busy space.

What should I look for in a tree company?

Years of experience, proper training and insurance, and a clear answer about what equipment they'll use and how they'll protect your yard.
